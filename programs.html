Programs & grants

The resources to turn possibility into progress.

Explore funds, programs and events. Each link connects directly to a verified CDF or Hall of Fame destination.

Explore what is available

Programs, grants and events in one place.

Cooperative Development Fund

U.S. cooperatives and groups that support them can propose research, development or technical assistance. CDF reviews concept notes first and invites selected applicants to submit a full proposal.

Review the grant process ↗

Cooperative Education Fund

CDF lists awards of $1,000–$10,000 for qualifying education and research work. The fund also sponsors eligible events. Applications go through CDF’s grant portal.

Review eligibility and apply ↗

Open CDF’s education grant application portal ↗

Disaster Recovery Fund

The fund helps cooperatives and their members recover from emergencies. CDF says all donations designated for the fund go toward recovery.

See recovery and application information ↗

Apply for disaster recovery funding ↗

Rural Cooperative Grant

CDF works with cooperative development organizations and regional partners to support rural businesses, jobs and access to essential services.

Explore the rural program ↗

Cooperative Leaders & Scholars

An eight-month fellowship for emerging cooperators that combines workshops, events and networking with peers from different co-op sectors.

Explore the fellowship ↗

Home Care Cooperative Initiative

CDF supports worker-owned home care through cooperative development resources and a national conference.

Explore home care resources ↗

Affordable Housing Initiative

CDF shares models and resources for resident-controlled and permanently affordable housing.

Explore housing resources ↗

Native American Food Economies

Conference sessions and training resources on how cooperatives can support Native food systems, agriculture and local economies.

Co-op 5K

CDF’s community run and walk raises funds for cooperative development and brings participants together in person and online.

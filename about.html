About CDF

Building a future rooted in shared ownership.

CDF supports the people and organizations strengthening cooperative enterprise, democratic decision-making and inclusive economic opportunity.

Mission and identity

Cooperation is a practical solution to shared challenges.

Established in 1944 and headquartered in Washington, DC, CDF is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit and the charitable affiliate of U.S. Co-ops.

Its work brings together grants, education, networks and programming to build capacity across the cooperative business community.

What is a cooperative?

A cooperative is a business owned and democratically controlled by the people who use its services or work in it. By bringing people together around shared needs, cooperative ownership can create resilient local institutions and build community wealth.

CDF’s history

CDF began in 1944 as the Freedom Fund. Its work expanded from postwar reconstruction to cooperative development in the U.S. and abroad.

1940s–1960s

The foundation helped launch the relief organization now known as CARE and later supported cooperative development and research through partnerships in India and the United States.

Explore CDF’s timeline ↗

1970s–1990s

CDF helped advance rural and senior cooperative housing and established events that continue to bring cooperators together.

Explore CDF’s timeline ↗

Today

CDF supports cooperative leadership, development and education, with priorities for stronger networks and local cooperative economies.
